Animai is an engine for turning ideas into high-quality animated educational videos. Write a scene in Python, bring it to life with reusable visual building blocks, and let the animation do the explaining.

From a sorting algorithm to a chemical reaction, you control what moves, what matters, and when the idea clicks. Charts, diagrams, maps, narration and timing all work together in one scene.

scene.pyPython
from animai import *

class AnIdea(AnimaiScene):
    def construct(self):
        bars = BarPlot([3, 1, 5, 2])
        self.show(bars)
        self.play(bars.grow())
